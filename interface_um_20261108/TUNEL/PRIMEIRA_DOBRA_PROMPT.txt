Wide cinematic 16:9 concept artwork for a scientific desktop experience. The moment of the first fold. A single minute incandescent ivory point embedded in a delicate, almost imperceptibly thin curved sheet of light; the sheet turns over like the caustic of folded glass and unfurls into a very deep, asymmetric three-dimensional cosmic web of warm pearl gold filaments and finer cool ice-blue galaxy concentrations, suspended in near-black navy space. One extraordinary central gesture: a single fine arc curling up from the point, then branching at different depths into small, luminous clusters of galaxies. The folds should read as structure resolving from one stroke, with genuine black voids between threads. A gorgeous physically rendered plate for a natural history museum, long-exposure astrophotography and understated scientific visualization. Sparse at left, rich textured filaments filling mid and right, quiet light fall-off around the edges, human sense of scale and silence, crisp details, dust mostly near the filaments. No lettering, no digits, no user interface, no panel or frame, no lens flare spikes, no planet, no spiral galaxy pasted on top, no grids or HUD. The interface supplies its own translatable title on the left.
